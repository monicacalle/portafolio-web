# Facts

Last updated: 2026-09-03

## What it is
The portfolio site of Monica Calle, a product designer (UX/UI) based in Valencia who
came to design from front-end development. It exists to get her interviews for product
design roles in Valencia and remote Europe.

## What it does not do
- She is early-career. The site does not claim years of agency experience.
- She has not led a design team, and the site does not imply that she has.
- The case studies are not written yet. Vibe and Voluntee are listed as projects, not
  yet as case studies with problem, solution and result. See `market-research.md` item 7.
- She has no published writing, so there is no back catalogue to link to.
