# Voice

Last updated: 2026-09-03
Source samples: none by the author. 3,142 words of borrowed reference prose from Frank
Chimero (frankchimero.com), Seth Godin (seths.blog) and Julie Zhuo (lg.substack.com),
saved in `ghost-drafts/voice-samples.md`. **This profile is provisional.** Monica is
early-career and has nothing published yet. Replace it with her own samples when she
has them. Decision and reasoning: `stages/01_research/output/voice-foundation-research.md`.

## Reader relationship
A designer explaining her decisions to another designer who is deciding whether to hire
her. Peer to peer, never applicant to gatekeeper.

## Three traits, each with its opposite
- Plain, not corporate
- Decided, not hedged
- Warm, not eager

## Measured from the samples (targets, not rules)
- Mean sentence length: 15 words; burstiness 0.72
- Paragraphs: short, 1 to 3 sentences
- Reading grade: 7.7
- Contractions: yes in English; natural register in Spanish
- Pronouns: I and you. The samples run 69 uses of "you" in 3,142 words
- Em dashes: never. Use a double hyphen, per the project guardrail. This overrides
  the samples, which run 3.8 per 1,000 words
- Emoji: never
- Exclamation marks: rare, under 1 per 1,000 words
- Three-item lists: at most 2 per 1,000 words. This is stricter than the samples on
  purpose. The essayists earn their triads over long paragraphs; in short portfolio copy
  the same pattern reads as padding, and it is the main fault in the current Spanish

## Signature moves
- Names the decision, then the reason for it, in that order.
- Admits a limit before anyone else can raise it.
- Uses one concrete noun where a category would do: "Figma", "Webflow", "un flujo de
  onboarding", never "soluciones digitales".
- Ends a section on a short line.

## Words they use
decision, reason, problem, constraint, tradeoff, shipped, tested, changed my mind,
what I would do differently, in practice, the boring part, it turned out

## Words they never use
Spanish only. The English slop list is the linter's own, so it is not repeated here.

apasionada, soluciones a medida, valor anadido, sinergia, experiencias memorables,
transformar ideas, impacto real, comprometida con la excelencia, pasion por el diseno,
enfoque holistico, potenciar, empoderar

## Two lines that sound right
> I wanted to take something big and make it small again.
> We only know for sure what it's like to be ourselves.

## One line that sounds wrong
> Disfruto transformar ideas en productos digitales que conecten con las personas,
> resuelvan necesidades reales y dejen una impresion memorable.

Why it is off-voice: it uses a three-verb list where one verb would carry the sentence,
every noun in it is abstract, and it makes no claim a reader could check. It is true of
every designer alive, so it tells a hiring manager nothing. From
`messages/es/about.json`, p3, on the rewrite list.
