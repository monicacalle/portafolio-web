# Audience

Last updated: 2026-09-03

## The one reader
A design lead or hiring manager at a product company in Valencia or remote Europe, the
kind of place Monica is applying to: Huspy, Shakers, Redbility, Vidext. They have an
open role and a stack of portfolios. They are reading on a phone, between meetings, and
they will give this site a couple of minutes at most. A good day for them is finding one
candidate worth a first call.

## Awareness and sophistication
Solution aware: they know they need a product designer and they are comparing people,
not deciding whether to hire. Sophistication is high. They have seen many junior
portfolios that open with a grid of pretty screens, so screens alone no longer move
them. What is scarce is evidence of thinking.

## Their words (verbatim, from sourced material)
### What they want
- "el product designer no solo se limita a pintar pantallas... esta muy cerca de lo que
  hace un Product Manager: analiza el mercado, analiza el negocio, analiza a los
  usuarios." (Mercadona Tech role description, GetManfred, via `market-research.md`)
### What they hate
- "If you're just slapping together components from a design system, you're already
  replaceable by AI." (Nielsen Norman Group, State of UX in 2026, via
  `market-research.md`)
### What they worry about
- That a junior hire will need more supervision than the role can absorb. (unverified:
  inferred from the NN/g finding that entry-level roles are scarce and competitive, not
  from a hiring manager directly)

## Objections and answers
| Objection | Answer (from facts.md) |
| --- | --- |
| Not enough years of experience | Seven shipped projects, two of them Next.js sites she built herself |
| Only makes screens | She came from front-end, so she validates what she designs before it is built |
| Design plus code reads as non-specialist | Untested for pure product roles (unverified). The hero wording should be able to dial this up or down. See `market-research.md` item 6.3 |
| Career changer | She has shipped work in both design and code, which is the thing being hired |

## Competitors as the reader describes them
Not applicable in the usual sense. Her competition is the other candidates in the stack,
and the reader does not describe them out loud. The NN/g line above is the closest thing
on record to how they are judged.
